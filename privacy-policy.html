Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to You

Our Privacy Policy is designed to protect personal information and ensure that it is kept secure. We take privacy seriously and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your data.

Any information collected from you will only be used for the purpose for which it was provided and will not be shared with any third parties without your consent. We value your trust and are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy while using our services.

What Data We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide including your name, contact details, and your child's name and age. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and to administer coaching sessions.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to respond to enquiries, to keep you informed about sessions and camps relevant to your child, and to administer your coaching relationship with C1 Coaching. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at enquiries@C1coaching.co.uk.
